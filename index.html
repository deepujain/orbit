Orbit

Playful skins for any web page. Turn one on with a single attribute, and your page becomes glass, crayon paper or the home of a cat. Record films a narrated tour of it in any skin.

Aura

Soft white glass by day, graphite by night. Cards catch a soft glow in their corners; buttons become pills.

₹1,36,520

Crayon

Cream ruled paper by day, a green chalkboard by night, with bunting, drifting clouds and a slow snail.

Whiskers

A cat that hops between your cards, rolls round buttons like wheels and naps. Tap it for a trick.

Record

An action, not a skin: its button films the page in whichever skin is on, ringing each part and captioning a line about it, with your voice if you give it one.

Use it on any page

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/deepujain/orbit/themes/aura.css">
<html data-orbit="aura">

Or from JavaScript

import { setTheme } from "orbit";
setTheme("whiskers");
